# CarePath AI v0.5 — Flagship Hackathon Demonstration

## Message

**The patient remains digitally visible throughout the healthcare journey.**

This demo uses synthetic data only.

## Primary synthetic patient

**Thandi Mokoena**  
Synthetic ID: `SYN-CP-0001`  
Preferred language: isiZulu  
Synthetic history includes hypertension, current medication and a documented penicillin allergy with provenance.

## Recommended demo sequence

### 1. Clinician enters CarePath

Sign in as `clinician` using the seeded demo password.

Show that the authenticated CarePath shell is role aware and that the user-type switcher is explicitly a hackathon demonstration feature.

### 2. Find the longitudinal record

Say:

> “Hey Ayanda, open Thandi Mokoena.”

Show:

- OneRecord;
- allergy and medication;
- conditions/results/encounters;
- source facility and recorded date;
- care-plan/document extensions.

Explain: CarePath assembles an authorised longitudinal view while preserving source provenance.

### 3. Show identity without inventing another national number

Say:

> “Hey Ayanda, open Consent and Identity.”

Open Thandi and show linked mock source identifiers.

Explain that the production architecture expects an authoritative MPI/identity service and governed merge/unmerge rather than name-only matching.

### 4. Create/coordinate the referral journey

Return to Referrals/Journey.

Create or review Thandi’s cardiology referral.

Show:

- referral reason;
- service;
- destination;
- state;
- human confirmation;
- synthetic capacity context;
- journey history.

### 5. Coordinate appointment and capacity

Open Appointment Management Centre.

Show the referral-linked booking and explain that admission-dependent bookings re-check the required synthetic bed type.

Open Facilities and show province/city filtering plus the full facility operational view.

### 6. Demonstrate patient transfer

If the scenario requires transport, open Ambulance & Transfers.

Show:

- available compatible ambulance;
- synthetic ETA;
- pickup/destination;
- referral linkage;
- destination bed dependency;
- transfer lifecycle.

State explicitly that this is not a live EMS/CAD/GPS feed.

### 7. Show CarePath Watch

Say:

> “Hey Ayanda, open CarePath Watch.”

Show a referral SLA warning and/or capacity constraint.

Explain the finding:

- triggering rule;
- evidence;
- owner;
- recommended administrative action.

Emphasise: it is operational early warning, not diagnosis.

### 8. Emergency access demonstration

Switch back to an emergency-capable clinical role if necessary.

Open **Emergency**.

Select Thandi, enter a specific emergency reason and re-authenticate with the demo password.

Show the minimum-necessary emergency summary and the 15-minute synthetic grant.

Then switch to `auditor` and demonstrate review of the Break Glass event through authorised governance/audit views.

### 9. National/provincial oversight

Switch to `manager`.

Say:

> “Hey Ayanda, open National Command.”

Show:

**South Africa → Province → District → City → Facility → Service**

Explain that management views are aggregate/de-identified by default.

### 10. Population health intelligence

Open Population Health.

Show synthetic province/service demand and observed referral outcomes.

State explicitly that raw recovery/mortality is not risk adjusted and is not a hospital-quality ranking.

### 11. Demonstrate interoperability

Say:

> “Hey Ayanda, open CarePath Exchange.”

Show:

`Mock source systems → CarePath Exchange → OneRecord + Journey`

Explain the FHIR R4-style demonstration endpoints for Patient, ServiceRequest and Appointment.

Do not claim formal conformance or live national integration.

### 12. Close the patient loop

Return to the patient journey.

Show specialist/referral outcome/feedback context and explain that the referring clinician should receive closure rather than losing the patient between institutions.

Switch to the patient role and show Health Passport, appointments, medication/vaccination, notifications and journey visibility.

## Closing statement

> **CarePath AI is not another isolated hospital system. It creates a trusted federated view and accountable care journey so the patient remains digitally visible from first contact through referral, treatment, follow-up and closure. One Patient. One Journey. One Trusted Health Record.**
